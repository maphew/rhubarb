Strain and cook down the juice to 1/2 cup. Puree the rhubarb in a food mill or a blender, or put through a vegetable mill. Beat the egg whites until they begin to stiffen, then add the remaining 1/2 cup sugar and the vanilla, continuing to beat until stiff peaks form.  Fold first the eggwhite mixture into the rhubarb, then the whipped cream. Make a collar of wax paper and fit it around a 1 1/2 quart souffle mold. Chill for at least six hours.
