There is a common theme within contemporary cultures, which dictates that in order for a person to be successful, they must stand out from the crowd.   
The easiest way to stand set yourself apart from everybody else - in a good way - is to use fashion.   
Clothes help to define who you are as a person, and it is possible to make all manner of statements through your sense of style.   
Whether you choose to wear the latest designer gear, or choose to wear an unusual choice of footwear, such as boat shoes, you can add twists to your outfit that ensure other people notice you.   
It is just a matter of knowing the rules and fortunately for you (if you want to stand out from the crowd), you have come to the right place.1.   
Remember, that in order to stand out, you do not look to other people and copy their style exactly.   
True, you can look for inspiration, but never 'steal' another person's look - particularly if he or she moves in the same circles as you.   
For instance, if your friend's favourite shoes happen to be a pair of funky boat shoes and you think they look great [Ralph Lauren Pas Cher](http://www.polodiscount.co), do not then purchase the same pair of shoes.   
Instead, look for a similar style or a different style by the same manufacturers.   
Clones never stand apart and if you wish to do so, direct copying is a no-no.2.   
It is important that you wear clothes that you are comfortable wearing.   
Look to the latest fashions, if you do not like a particular style of clothing, do not wear it.   
Likewise, do not choose to wear styles that you do not like, but which other people will find unusual, just to stand out - you may generate the desired effect, but it will cost you your sense of confidence.   
Truly fashionable people where clothes that make them feel good, which in turn are clothes they like.3.   
Understand which styles you suit.   
In general, if you like a particular style, then you will probably suit that style, hence the reason you favour it.   
People are normally attracted to things they are good at and things that they suit, so if you feel drawn to clothes or shoes, then they are probably right for you.   
For instance, Irregular Choice shoes are innovative and unique, many people who wear them love to dress in an individual manner, other people, however, are scared of standing out to the degree that these shoes allow, but if you find yourself attracted to Irregular Choice shoes, then you should certainly consider buying them.4.   
Never dress according to other people's rules.
