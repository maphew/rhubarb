Dan,

You are correct. I mislabeled my weevil. This weevil is not a Boll Weevil. I do believe
this is in fact the weevil you are looking for.....Lixus concavus. Let me explain.
Properly speaking Rhubarb curculio is not a correct scientific name. Lixus concavus is the
Rhubarb Weevil. The Rhubarb Weevil is similar to the Boll Weevil and Pine Weevil but it is
larger (1/2"-3/8" for the Rhubarb weevil -vs- 1/4" for the boll and pine
weevils). I am about 99.9% sure that the picture in question is indeed a Rhubarb Weevil
and not a Boll Weevil. I usually do not misidentify with scientific names, but common
names are a dime a dozen. If I were you go to the library and get some entomology books or
other insect books to make your final decision. I am not an entomologist either, it is
just a hobby. I am, like I said previously almost positive my picture is a rhubarb weevil.
So much so that I have changed the name on my web page. Thank you for bringing this to my
attention as well as I hope you can benefit. The Boll weevil is Anthonomus grandis. Feel
free to use my picture if you need....

Good Luck -Jamie
